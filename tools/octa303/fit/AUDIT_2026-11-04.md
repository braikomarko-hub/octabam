## Open

- **tinyloops** plays 35.70 Hz in 4,941-sample steps (4.000 cycles a step, measured), not 35.42 Hz. Every real note
  meets the saw at one phase; ringtrack's onset lands on a saw reset some time after the note starts. That time is
  not recoverable from the recording: the fundamental's phase put the twin in the wrong onset regime (review 2),
  and the energy trough before the onset scatters by hundreds of samples on a twin with the recording's noise
  floor (tdelay.py). With the phase free the twin's error spans 0.04–0.64 oct, wider than the versions differ.
  `fit75 --tiny` refuses until a phase-independent measure exists.
- The early windows (4–40 ms) carry the VCO's phase, random in the recordings: cell40 is the phase-free part.
