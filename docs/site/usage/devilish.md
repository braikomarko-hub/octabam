Robin Whittle's **Devil Fish** modifications to the TB-303, for
[OCTA-303](octa303.md) tracks. Put dEVILish on FX1 or FX2 of an OCTA-303
track: the voice reads its twelve knobs and applies them inside the 303
circuit (oscillator level into the filter, filter FM, tracking, envelopes,
slide). Every default is the plain TB-303; on any other track it does
nothing.

## Controls

| Page | Knob | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | OVDR | overdrive: the VCO into the filter (25 = the 303) |
| 1 | FM | filter FM from the VCA output |
| 1 | TRCK | filter tracking |
| 1 | VDEC | volume decay, up to endless notes |
| 1 | SLID | slide time, 60–360 ms |
| 1 | ADEC | accented notes' filter decay, 30 ms–3 s |
| 2 | SATK | soft attack on non-accented notes |
| 2 | SWP | ACID (the 303), WHST, SELF (the filter can self-oscillate) |
| 2 | SPD | accent sweep speed: FAST, NORM, SLOW |
| 2 | MUFF | the Muffler: OFF, SOFT, HARD |
| 2 | CUTX | the wide cutoff range |
| 2 | ENVX | env mod tripled |

Page 1 follows locks, scenes and the crossfader like any effect's knobs.
